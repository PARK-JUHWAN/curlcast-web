Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Overview

CurlCast ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

CurlCast does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data you enter (hair profile, wash day logs, preferences) is stored locally on your device only.

Location Data

CurlCast uses your device's approximate location solely to fetch current dew point data from a public weather API (Open-Meteo). Your coordinates are sent directly to Open-Meteo to retrieve weather data. We do not store, log, or transmit your location to any other server. Location data is never linked to your identity.

What We Don't Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or account information
  • No health or medical data
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No cookies or advertising identifiers
  • No photos or camera access

Local Storage

The app stores your hair profile (porosity, curl pattern), wash day logs, weather cache, and notification preferences locally on your device using Hive (local database). This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all stored data.

In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details. Subscription management is handled through your Apple ID settings.

Push Notifications

The app uses local notifications only (scheduled on your device). No data is sent to any push notification server. You can enable or disable notifications in the app's settings.

Third-Party Services

  • Open-Meteo: Used to fetch dew point and weather forecast data. Open-Meteo receives your approximate coordinates for weather lookup. See their terms.
  • RevenueCat: Used for subscription management. RevenueCat may collect anonymous transaction data as described in their privacy policy. No personally identifiable information is shared.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
